    How it works

    1. Claude reads each photo you add and fills in its category, colour and style.
    2. Claude styles it for you. For a piece you are thinking of buying, it checks your closet and gives a straight verdict, any duplicates, and up to three outfits.
    3. YouCam puts it on you. Tops use the YouCam AI Clothes try-on and shoes use its AI Shoes try-on, one after the other, so each result feeds the next.

    Your photos and closet stay in this browser. The server keeps nothing.

    Which shoes should we show?

    Shoe try-on uses men's or women's styles. Pick the ones you'd like to see on you. You can change this later.
